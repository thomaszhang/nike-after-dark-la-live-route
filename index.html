Before your run
  1. Live tracking starts automatically. Allow precise location when Safari asks.
  2. Keep this page open while running. Add it to your Home Screen for one-tap access.
  3. The blue dot is you. Distance, remaining distance, elevation, and status stay at the top.
  4. The bottom navigation card combines your next course turn and live heading direction.
  5. Tap Route or Location once to select that view. Tap the selected view again to toggle its Direction sub-button. Dragging the map leaves both views unselected.
  6. Rotate the map manually with two fingers. Manual rotation turns Direction off.
  7. Route Direction applies while sliding across the elevation profile. Location Direction rotates the live map to your heading. Releasing the profile restores your previous view.

iPhone may pause browser location after the screen locks. This page requests that the screen stay awake when supported.
