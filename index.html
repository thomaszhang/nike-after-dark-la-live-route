Before your run
  1. Tap Start live tracking and allow precise location.
  2. Keep this page open while running. Add it to your Home Screen for one-tap access.
  3. The blue dot is you. The card shows course progress and how far you are from the race line.
  4. Tap Heading to rotate the street map and route in your running direction. GPS movement leads while you run; the phone compass fills in when you slow down.
  5. The top banner shows the next course turn and updates as you move. Direct cues pause if you are more than about 330 ft from the course; use Full route to choose a safe way back.

iPhone may pause browser location after the screen locks. This page requests that the screen stay awake when supported.
